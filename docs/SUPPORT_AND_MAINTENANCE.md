# Proposed Support and Maintenance Model

This model is a proposal for discussion. Response targets and final service levels are subject to agreement with the Client during contracting and depend on the agreed support coverage hours.

## Support categories

| Priority | Examples | Proposed acknowledgement | Proposed workaround/action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Website unavailable, security incident affecting the site, all authorised administrators unable to sign in, core repository/download function unusable | Within 2 hours during support coverage | Workaround within 4 hours where reasonably achievable |
| High | Major CMS feature unavailable, significant broken functionality, major browser compatibility issue | Within 4 business hours | Resolution or workaround within 1 business day where reasonably achievable |
| Routine | Minor UI issue, non-critical defect, formatting issue, ordinary CMS question | Within 1 business day | Schedule into the maintenance queue |

## Routine monthly maintenance

- Bug fixes and compatibility review across supported browsers.
- Dependency, framework, CMS and security updates, with release review.
- Backup job monitoring and restore-readiness checks.
- Basic uptime, performance, TLS certificate and storage-capacity review.
- Error-log review and analytics/integration health checks.
- CMS questions and editorial workflow support.

## Enhancements and backups

Routine maintenance covers operation of agreed functionality. New features, new map or dashboard types, substantive redesigns, major workflow changes, new third-party integrations and extensive data migrations are future enhancements; scope and price them separately with prior Client approval.

Proposed backup baseline: nightly database backup, daily/incremental file protection, weekly server snapshot, 30 daily and 12 monthly retention points, an off-site copy, and quarterly restore verification. Agree final schedule and retention with the Client and selected host. This system is not deployed by the prototype.
