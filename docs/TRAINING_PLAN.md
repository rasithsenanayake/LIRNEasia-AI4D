# Proposed Administrator and Editor Training Plan

Plan for a small Client administrator/editor team. Training is proposed and has not yet taken place. Deliver remotely or in another agreed format using a practical demonstration environment.

## Session 1 — CMS foundations

- Login and account security; dashboard and roles.
- Create and edit records; draft, preview and publish.
- Media and files; archive and unpublish.

## Session 2 — Structured content

- Publications, use cases, datasets, people and organisations.
- Events, learning resources and opportunities.
- Topics and taxonomy; metadata quality and relationships.

## Session 3 — Data and engagement

- Dataset updates, import workflow and validation.
- Map and data updates; gated downloads and engagement exports.
- Newsletter integration concepts and analytics interpretation.

## Session 4 — Administration

- Users and permissions; content quality and accessibility.
- SEO metadata; backup and recovery awareness; escalation paths.

## Materials and follow-up

Provide an administrator guide, editor quick-start guide, analytics guide, deployment/technical guide, and backup/recovery guide. A recorded session can be supplied if agreed. Include follow-up or refresher support as agreed with the Client.
